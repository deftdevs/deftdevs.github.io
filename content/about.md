Deft Devs is an independent DevOps and software engineering consultancy. When you work with Deft Devs, you work directly with its founder, without account managers or handovers.

## Patrick Hobusch, Founder

![Patrick Hobusch](/images/patrick-hobusch.jpg)

Patrick has been a software engineer since 2013 and has worked in DevOps since 2017. He holds an M.Sc. in Computer Science from Freie Universität Berlin.

Before founding Deft Devs in 2022, he was a Principal DevOps Engineer at a consultancy serving large enterprises. There he led cloud migrations to Microsoft Azure, built CI/CD toolchains for automotive suppliers, and created open-source tools for configuring Atlassian applications as code. Most recently he served as contract CTO of an AI startup, taking its product from prototype to production. His upstream contributions include Helmfile, Renovate, NixOS and several widely used Helm charts.

> I believe in sustainable software solutions that guarantee a high development pace in the long term, rather than quick-and-dirty hacks that give the illusion of fast results during project starts. I'm known for preferring standards over custom solutions, contributing to open source projects, implementing clean code and striving for high levels of automation, including automated tests. I'm capable of designing high-level software and infrastructure architectures, as well as digging into implementation details. My clients can expect real consultation from me and not just the unquestioned ticking off of to-dos.
